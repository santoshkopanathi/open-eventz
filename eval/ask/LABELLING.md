**How to fill this in.** For each question, write what a careful person would do, using the
[event sheet](event-sheet.md). Don't look at any model output first: these are the answers the
model will be graded against. Short answers are fine. "Not sure" is a fine answer too: say why in Notes.

- **Dates:** the from and to dates you think the question means (e.g. Nov 21 – Nov 21).
- **City:** Frisco, Plano, or both. Remember the tab: with no city named, the tab decides.
- **Free only:** yes only if the parent asked for free.
- **Age:** the age or range the parent gave, or "none".
- **Show:** up to 5 Refs (E###) a careful person would show, in order, or "none", or "redirect" for off-topic.
  If more than 5 fit equally, list the 5 you would pick and say so in Notes.
